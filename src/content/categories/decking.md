## Deck Building Calculators

A well-planned deck starts with accurate footing and material estimates. Undersized footings cause settlement; undersized material orders cause work stoppages. Our deck calculators help you get both right before the first shovel hits the ground.

## Deck Calculator Tools

**Deck Footing Calculator** — The most critical deck calculation. Enter post count, load area, and your soil bearing capacity to get footing diameter and concrete volume per footing. Outputs total cubic feet and bag count for all footings combined.

## Deck Footing Basics

Deck footings must extend below the frost line in your climate zone. For most of the northern US, that's 36–42 inches. Southern states may only require 12–18 inches.

**Standard footing diameters:**
- Light loads (small deck, 6 ft spans): 10–12 inch diameter
- Medium loads (typical residential deck): 12–16 inch diameter  
- Heavy loads (hot tub, large cantilever): 16–24 inch diameter

Always check your local building code — most jurisdictions require a permit for decks over 200 sq ft or attached to the house.

## Footing Concrete Volume

For a 12-inch diameter footing at 42 inches deep, you need approximately **0.327 cubic feet** of concrete — about half an 80 lb bag. Most deck projects use **10–16 footings**, putting total concrete at 3–8 cubic feet. Bags are usually the most cost-effective option at this scale.

Use our [Concrete Bags Calculator](/calculators/concrete-bags-calculator/) alongside the footing calculator to get your total bag count.

## Post Sizing Guidelines

| Deck Height | Recommended Post Size |
|-------------|----------------------|
| Under 8 ft | 4×4 (light loads) or 6×6 |
| 8–14 ft | 6×6 |
| Over 14 ft | 6×6 or engineered post |

For decks over 10 feet above grade, consult a structural engineer for post and beam sizing.
