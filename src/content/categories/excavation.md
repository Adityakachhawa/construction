## Excavation and Fill Material Calculators

Every outdoor project starts with the ground. Whether you're grading a yard, laying a gravel driveway, adding topsoil to a garden bed, or digging a French drain, knowing exact material volumes prevents costly over-ordering and shortfalls on delivery day.

Our excavation calculators handle everything from simple volume math to complex drainage systems.

## Excavation Calculator Tools

**Gravel Calculator** — The most-used calculator in this category. Enter area and depth to get cubic yards of gravel, crushed stone, or base material. Works for driveways, walkways, patios, and drainage layers.

**Excavation Calculator** — Calculate the volume of dirt to remove for a foundation, pool, or grade cut. Outputs cubic yards for hauling estimates and fill comparisons.

**Topsoil Calculator** — How many cubic yards (or truckloads) of topsoil for a lawn, garden bed, or raised bed? Enter square footage and depth.

**Sand Calculator** — Volume and weight for bedding sand under pavers, sandbox fill, or concrete mix aggregate.

**Mulch Calculator** — Cubic yards of mulch for garden beds. Standard mulch depth is 2–3 inches; 3–4 inches for weed suppression.

**Aggregate Calculator** — General-purpose tool for any crushed aggregate material. Outputs volume and estimated weight.

**French Drain Calculator** — Linear footage of pipe, gravel volume for the trench, and fabric area for any drainage run.

**Square Footage Calculator** — Quick area calculator for any rectangular space. Foundation for all other volume calculations.

## Gravel and Fill Material Types

| Material | Typical Use | Weight per CY |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| Crushed limestone | Driveways, base layers | 1.4–1.6 tons |
| Pea gravel | Drainage, landscaping | 1.3–1.4 tons |
| River rock | Decorative, drainage | 1.3–1.5 tons |
| Crushed concrete | Base fill | 1.4–1.5 tons |
| Clean fill dirt | Grading, backfill | 1.1–1.4 tons |
| Topsoil | Lawn, garden | 0.9–1.1 tons |

## Material Delivery Sizes

- **Pickup truck:** 0.5–1 cubic yard
- **Small dump trailer:** 2–3 cubic yards
- **Single-axle dump truck:** 5–7 cubic yards
- **Tandem dump truck:** 10–14 cubic yards

Calculate your volume first, then divide by typical truck capacity to estimate delivery count.

## French Drain Planning

A French drain trench is typically 12–18 inches wide and 18–24 inches deep. The trench is filled with clean crushed stone (3/4" to 1.5") around a perforated pipe. Our [French Drain Calculator](/calculators/french-drain-calculator/) estimates stone volume, pipe footage, and fabric area for the entire run.
