## Framing Calculators for Walls, Roofs, and Floors

Framing is the structural skeleton of every building. Whether you're framing a new wall, calculating rafter lengths for a shed roof, or estimating lumber for a room addition, accurate material counts prevent waste and keep your project on schedule.

Our framing calculators cover the full range of structural lumber work — from 16" OC wall studs to complex rafter cuts.

## Framing Calculator Tools

**Stud Calculator** — How many 2×4 or 2×6 studs for a wall? Enter wall length, stud spacing (16" or 24" OC), and the tool accounts for corners, openings, and top/bottom plates.

**Rafter Calculator** — Calculate rafter length, ridge height, bird's mouth cut dimensions, and total board feet based on span and roof pitch. Works for gable, shed, and hip roofs.

**Roof Pitch Calculator** — Convert rise-over-run to degrees, calculate slope multipliers, and find rafter length from run. Essential before ordering any roofing material.

**Roofing Calculator** — Estimate shingles (squares), underlayment, and ridge cap for any roof shape. Use after the Roof Pitch Calculator to get your surface area.

**Metal Roofing Calculator** — Calculate metal panel coverage, trim lengths, and fastener quantities. Works for standing seam and corrugated profiles.

**Drywall Calculator** — How many 4×8 or 4×12 drywall sheets for a room? Handles multiple walls, windows, and doors with 10% waste built in.

**Plywood Calculator** — Sheet count for subfloors, wall sheathing, or roof decking. Adjusts for standard sheet sizes and waste.

**Lumber Calculator** — Board count, linear footage, and board feet for any framing dimension. Use for joist packages, header stock, and material orders.

**Board Foot Calculator** — The standard lumber pricing unit. Convert dimensions to board feet for any species and size.

**Siding Calculator** — Coverage for lap siding, T1-11, and board-and-batten. Accounts for overlap and waste.

**Stair Calculator** — Rise, run, total staircase dimensions, and stringer length. Checks against IRC code limits for riser height and tread depth.

## Lumber Sizing Quick Reference

| Nominal | Actual |
|---------|--------|
| 2×4 | 1.5" × 3.5" |
| 2×6 | 1.5" × 5.5" |
| 2×8 | 1.5" × 7.25" |
| 2×10 | 1.5" × 9.25" |
| 2×12 | 1.5" × 11.25" |

Always order by **actual dimension** when calculating load capacity, but use **nominal** when ordering from the lumber yard.

## Standard Framing Practices

Wall studs are typically spaced **16" on center** for load-bearing walls and **24" on center** for non-load-bearing partitions. Roof rafters follow the same spacing conventions.

A standard 8-foot wall with 16" OC stud spacing needs approximately **1 stud per linear foot** of wall — a reliable rule of thumb for quick estimates.
