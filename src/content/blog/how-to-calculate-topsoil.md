Topsoil is the foundation of any healthy lawn or garden. Knowing how much to order prevents the expense of delivery shortages and the inconvenience of a mountain of leftover soil in your driveway.

## What Is Topsoil?

Topsoil is the upper 4–12 inches of soil, rich in organic matter, microorganisms, and nutrients. It's the growing zone for plants. Below topsoil is subsoil — denser, less fertile, and used for structural fill, not growing.

> **Warning:** Never use topsoil as structural fill under driveways, patios, foundations, or any load-bearing surface. Topsoil contains organic matter that compresses and decomposes over time — causing settlement cracks and surface failure. Use compactable gravel or engineered fill for structural applications.

## How to Calculate Topsoil Volume

**Volume (cubic feet) = Area (sq ft) × Depth (ft)**
**Volume (cubic yards) = Volume (cubic feet) ÷ 27**

### Topsoil Depth Guide

| Application | Recommended Depth |
|------------|------------------|
| Lawn establishment | 4–6 inches |
| Lawn overseeding | 1–2 inches |
| Garden bed | 8–12 inches |
| Raised bed (new) | 12–18 inches |
| Top-dressing | 0.5–1 inch |

### Coverage Chart (Cubic Yards per Area)

| Area | 4" Deep | 6" Deep | 12" Deep |

| 1,000 sq ft | 12.3 yd³ | 18.5 yd³ | 37 yd³ |
| 2,000 sq ft | 24.7 yd³ | 37 yd³ | 74 yd³ |

## Delivery Options

- **By the bag** (40 lb, ~0.75 cu ft): Only for very small areas. Expensive per yard.
- **Pickup truck load:** 0.5–1 cubic yard. Good for small projects.
- **Bulk delivery:** Most suppliers deliver in 5–15 yard loads. Minimum delivery varies.

## Topsoil Quality

Not all topsoil is equal. Ask suppliers:
- Is it screened (no rocks or debris)?
- What's the organic matter content?
- Has it been tested for weed seeds?

"Screened topsoil" is worth the premium for most applications.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How many cubic yards in a pickup truck load?**
A full-size pickup carries roughly 0.5 cubic yards (half a ton) comfortably. Don't exceed your truck's payload rating — topsoil weighs about 1 ton per cubic yard.

**Can I mix topsoil with my existing soil?**
Yes — in fact, tilling new topsoil into your existing soil (to 6–8 inches) produces better results than simply layering it on top, which can cause drainage stratification.

**How much does topsoil cost?**
Bulk delivered topsoil runs $25–$75 per cubic yard depending on quality and your region. Screened, blended topsoil commands the higher end. Delivery charges add $50–$150 for most areas.
